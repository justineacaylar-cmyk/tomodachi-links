STRAWBERRY

★ ＴＯＰ ５ ★
From late December to May, greenhouse farms near Tokyo throw open the doors for all-you-can-eat ichigo-gari.
NO.1
Tanakaen 田中園
40-min all-you-can-eat, 10+ kinds ・ Funabashi, Chiba (Misaki Stn) ・ ¥¥ Easy Tokyo day-trip
A Chiba greenhouse with 40-min all-you-can-eat across 10+ premium varieties; an easy winter day-trip near Misaki Station. Jan–May.
02
Yoshimura Strawberry Park 吉村いちご園
No-time-limit all-you-can-eat ・ Mashiko, Tochigi (Kitayama Stn) ・ ¥¥ Rare unlimited-time picking
Huge range (Tochiotome, Skyberry, Milkyberry…) and no time cap on picking. Mid-Dec to mid-May.
03
Sayama Berryland サヤマベリーランド
Pick-your-own greenhouse ・ Sayama/Kawagoe, Saitama (Shin-Sayama Stn) ・ ¥¥ Closest big farm north of Tokyo
Saitama favorite open mid-Dec to late May, finishing with house-made strawberry juice and ichigo kakigori.
04
Ichigo-no-Sato いちごの里
All-you-can-eat Tochiotome ・ Oyama, Tochigi (Oyama Stn) ・ ¥¥ Heart of the 'Strawberry Kingdom'
In Japan's top strawberry prefecture; offers several cultivars including prized Tochiotome, with a farm shop and café. ~Dec–May.
05
Tokue Strawberry Farm とくえ農園
Greenhouse all-you-can-eat ・ Aoba-ku, Yokohama (Ichigao Stn) ・ ¥¥ ~1 hour from Shibuya
One of the closest ichigo-gari to central Tokyo — about an hour from Shibuya on the Den-en-toshi Line. Jan–May.
