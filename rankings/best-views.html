BEST VIEWS

★ ＴＯＰ ５ ★
The jaw-dropping vistas — from Mt Fuji framed by a pagoda to a glowing blue carpet of a million flowers.
NO.1
Chureito Pagoda & Mt Fuji 忠霊塔・富士山
Pagoda view of Mt Fuji ・ Yamanashi (Shimo-Yoshida Stn) ・ Free Japan's most iconic Fuji view
A five-story pagoda at Arakura Sengen Shrine framing Mt Fuji with cherry blossoms — arguably Japan's most photographed view.
02
Shibuya Sky 渋谷スカイ
Open-air rooftop deck ・ Tokyo (Shibuya Stn) ・ ¥3,000+ Tokyo's premier 360° view
A 230m open-air rooftop above the world's busiest crossing — sweeping sunset panoramas, Mt Fuji on clear days.
03
Arashiyama Bamboo Grove 嵐山竹林
Towering bamboo path ・ Kyoto (Saga-Arashiyama Stn) ・ Free Kyoto's signature landmark
A serene path through soaring bamboo on Kyoto's west edge, filtered green light and rustling leaves overhead.
04
Hitachi Seaside Park 国営ひたち海浜公園
Vast seaside flower park ・ Ibaraki (Katsuta Stn + bus) ・ ¥450+ World-famous nemophila & kochia
Late spring: a sea of 5M+ sky-blue nemophila on Miharashi Hill; autumn: the same hills blaze crimson with kochia.
05
Takachiho Gorge 高千穂峡
Volcanic river gorge ・ Miyazaki, Kyushu (bus to Takachiho) ・ Free (boat ~¥5,000) National Place of Scenic Beauty
Sheer basalt columns where Manai Falls plunges into emerald water — best seen by rowboat beneath the cliffs.
