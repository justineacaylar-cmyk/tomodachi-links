NO.1
Himeji Castle 姫路城
Original keep, 'White Heron' ・ Hyogo (Himeji Stn) ・ ¥2,500 UNESCO; National Treasure
Japan's finest surviving feudal castle (1609); brilliant white walls earned it the name Shirasagi-jo, White Egret Castle.

02
Matsumoto Castle 松本城
Original keep, 'Crow Castle' ・ Nagano (Matsumoto Stn) ・ ¥1,300 National Treasure; original 12
One of Japan's oldest keeps (c.1593–94); striking black walls and a red bridge against the Northern Alps.
🎫 Tours & tickets ›
03
Hikone Castle 彦根城
Original keep + castle town ・ Shiga (Hikone Stn) ・ ¥800 National Treasure; original 12
Completed 1622, never destroyed; retains its keep, daimyo garden (Genkyu-en) and samurai-era streets.
🎫 Tours & tickets ›
04
Osaka Castle 大阪城
Reconstructed keep (museum) ・ Osaka (Osakajokoen Stn) ・ ¥1,200 Symbol of Osaka
Originally built 1583 by Toyotomi Hideyoshi; the 1931 keep houses a museum, ringed by huge original stone walls.
🎫 Tours & tickets ›
05
Nijo Castle 二条城
Flatland palace-castle ・ Kyoto (Nijojo-mae Stn) ・ ¥1,300 UNESCO World Heritage
Built 1603 as the shogun's Kyoto residence; famed for 'nightingale' chirping floors and gilded sliding-door art.
