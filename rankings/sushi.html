TOKYO SUSHI

★ ＴＯＰ ５ ★
From three-Michelin-star altars to lightning-fast ¥120 plates — five legendary Tokyo counters.
NO.1
Sukiyabashi Jiro すきやばし次郎
Edomae omakase, ultra high-end ・ Ginza (by Ginza Stn) ・ ¥¥¥ Former 3⭐ (no public booking)
The ~10-seat counter of Jiro Ono, the 'god of sushi' made famous by Jiro Dreams of Sushi.
02
Sushi Saito 鮨さいとう
Edomae omakase, ultra high-end ・ Roppongi (Ark Hills South Tower) ・ ¥¥¥ Former 3⭐ (invitation-only)
Rated among the world's very best; Takashi Saito's intimate counter is near-impossible to book.
03
Manten Sushi まんてん鮨
Edomae omakase, best value ・ Marunouchi (by Tokyo Stn) ・ ¥¥ Acclaimed value omakase
A chef's omakase that brings counter-grade sushi without the months-long wait of the three-star giants.
04
Sushi Dai 寿司大
Market breakfast omakase ・ Toyosu Market (Shijo-mae Stn) ・ ¥¥ Toyosu's famous market sushi
Toyosu's most famous market sushi; the dawn omakase draws hours-long queues from early morning.
05
Uobei Shibuya 魚べい
High-speed touch-panel sushi ・ Shibuya Dogenzaka ・ ¥
Genki Sushi's playful 'rocket' sushi — order on a tablet, plates zoom to your seat from ~¥120.
